Issue #1 Review patch ↗

Growing sort buffers can stall a frame

Stock Radix copies zero-filled arrays into six new scratch buffers during the sort. The patch allocates the same six buffers directly on the GPU.

Recorded comparison

RTX 3090 · Edge 157 · Roman Parish

Stock sort42.2 ms
Patched sort1.3 ms

Growing from 2M to 3M candidates delayed the next callback to 46.1 ms on stock and 16.5 ms with the patch. Replaying 3M or increasing to 3.5M inside the new capacity took 0.3 ms on stock.
